## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, sovereign, and deeply grounded visual identity tailored for an African environmental engineering leader. Anchored across the Sahel and central river basins (Niger, Burkina Faso, Nigeria, Burundi, Chad), the design aesthetic transitions ecological remediation into sovereign industrial value: converting invasive water hyacinth into circular bioproducts.

The UI philosophy reflects:
- **Scientific Rigor & Authority:** Calm, deliberate architectural layouts built for institutional stakeholders, development banks, government ministries, and international research consortiums.
- **Ecological Mineralism:** A departure from typical consumer-facing tech trends. Strictly avoiding superficial neon greens, glassmorphic blurs, and playful rounded blobs. The system privileges warm bone-ivory backgrounds, deep forest depths, rich earth ochre accents, and crisp technical hairline borders.
- **Editorial Dignity:** Balances precise metric tables and engineering schemas with human-centered, narrative declarations from leadership using refined editorial serif accents.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from riverine ecology, mineral soil, and dense vegetative biomass:

- **Primary (`#173A2C` — Forêt Profonde):** Structural anchors, authoritative navigation headers, high-impact brand statements, and foundational dark containers.
- **Secondary (`#3F6B45` — Vert Vivant):** Active states, interactive indicators, secondary CTAs, scientific verification badges, and positive ecological indicators.
- **Accent / Tertiary (`#B78A4A` — Ocre Organique):** Technical milestones, metadata labels, agricultural indices, field deployment markers, and specialized editorial flags.
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F3EC` — Ivoire Chaud):** Primary background offering warm paper-like comfort that minimizes digital fatigue.
- **Surface Elevation (`#E9E4D8` — Sable Naturel):** Distinct secondary surface for card panels, technical data blocks, and sidebars.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity focal cards and input backgrounds.
- **Text Primary (`#202522` — Anthracite Minéral):** High legibility body text and data values.
- **Text Muted (`#68716B`):** Supporting technical descriptors, breadcrumbs, and captions.
- **Structural Hairlines (`#D7D9D2`):** Subtle boundaries and grid partitions with minimal visual weight.

## Typography

Typography establishes an interplay between industrial precision and executive narrative:

- **Headlines (DM Sans):** Engineered, clean, geometric sans-serif set with tighter letter tracking at large scales to project institutional clarity and technical precision.
- **Body & Data (Inter):** Neutral, hyper-legible workhorse for reports, technical metrics, impact tables, and multi-column documentations.
- **Leadership & Editorial Voice (Source Serif 4):** Reserved exclusively for high-level quotes, mission manifestos, and statements by the founder. It anchors the platform with human resonance, gravity, and intellectual authority.
- **Labels & Metrics (DM Sans, Uppercase Tracking):** Used for technical metadata tags, location identifiers (e.g., *NIAMEY — BASSIN DU FLEUVE NIGER*), and industrial patent references.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture is grounded in generous breathing room, modular 12-column grids, and asymmetric editorial sections that echo physical environmental reports:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and outer canvas padding scaling up to 48px (`3rem`). Content sections emphasize deliberate asymmetric offsets—pairing 7-column analytical narratives with 5-column technical metric dossiers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters. Content blocks collapse into structured vertical groupings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) lateral margins. Full horizontal bleed for data visualization strips and touch-friendly metric tables.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Controlled by standard vertical intervals: components (`space-md`), intra-group content (`space-lg`), and section transitions (`space-xl` to multiples thereof).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating drop shadows, diffuse glows, or skeuomorphic bevels. Elevation is achieved through structural planar layering and crisp delineation:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces stack without simulated light sources. Base page is `#F5F3EC`, structural sub-sections utilize `#E9E4D8`, and foreground data plates utilize crisp `#FFFFFF`. High-contrast institutional panels deploy `#173A2C`.
- **Structural Outlines:** Depth boundaries are defined using deliberate 1px hairline rules in `#D7D9D2`. Cards and data cells sit flush against each other or separated by consistent gutters with hairline dividers.
- **Active / Focused Depth:** Hover states and focal shifts are indicated by border color shifts to `#3F6B45` or subtle interior hairline frames rather than directional elevation changes.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses architectural restraint and technical maturity:

- **Micro-Radii (2px - 4px maximum):** Applied across all interactive buttons, cards, inputs, and analytical panels. This replaces standard playful software pill shapes with the crisp, calibrated finish of an industrial instrument or technical dossier.
- **Interior Insets:** Badges and metric callouts follow precise 2px corners, preserving structural harmony with parent containers.
- **Dividers & Delimiters:** Sharp 90-degree orthogonal line ends for process charts, circular economy flows, and data graphs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#173A2C`, text `#F5F3EC`, 2px micro-rounded corners. Hover: Background shifts to `#3F6B45`. Active: `#122C21`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, 1px solid border in `#173A2C`, text `#173A2C`. Hover: Background fills `#173A2C` at 6% opacity.
- **Technical / Accent:** Background `#B78A4A`, text `#FFFFFF`. Applied strictly for impact reports, patent specs, and direct downloads.
- **Structure:** Padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`, typography `DM Sans` 14px weight 600, uppercase letter-spacing `0.04em`.

### Data Cards & Dossiers
- **Structure:** Crisp background `#FFFFFF` or `#E9E4D8`, bounded by 1px `#D7D9D2` border. 2px corner radius.
- **Asymmetric Header:** Left-aligned technical category tag in `label-md` using `#B78A4A`, followed by DM Sans headline and Inter data metrics.
- **Footer:** Hairline rule top border separating metadata (date, territory: Niger/Chad Basin, verification status).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Technology Indicators:** Micro-radius 2px, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`.
- **JaciGrow™ / Biofertilizer:** Pale sage fill `#E6ECE5`, text `#173A2C`, border `#C6D4C5`.
- **JaciBiogaz™ / Energy:** Pale warm amber fill `#F7F2E9`, text `#B78A4A`, border `#DEC9A8`.
- **JaciPur'O™ / Water Technology:** Neutral grey-tinted green fill with `#3F6B45` type.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Container:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#D7D9D2`, 2px radius. Height 44px for field stability.
- **Focus:** 1px solid `#173A2C`, no outer glow or drop shadow.
- **Labels:** Positioned above the field in `Inter` 13px weight 500, color `#202522`. Supporting micro-text in `#68716B`.

### Lists & Technical Tables
- **Lists:** Clean typographic rows separated by 1px hairlines in `#D7D9D2`. No bullet points; replaced with small 4px square markers in `#B78A4A` or sequential numeric counters in DM Sans bold.
- **Data Tables:** Dense horizontal rows, `#F5F3EC` alternate striping, headers in DM Sans 12px uppercase `#68716B` with bottom border 1.5px `#173A2C`.

### Editorial Quotation Blocks
- **Format:** Inset card with 3px left border in `#B78A4A`, background `#E9E4D8`.
- **Typography:** `Source Serif 4` 20px-24px italic for Mariama Mamane's declarations, followed by attribution in `DM Sans` 12px uppercase tracking.
